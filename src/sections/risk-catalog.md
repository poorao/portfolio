<div class="banner-callout banner-tip-purple">
    <div class="banner-callout-icon">📘</div>
    <div class="banner-callout-content">
        <strong>The 460,000 Model Cards Audit &amp; RiskRAG</strong>
        <p>
            By auditing nearly 460,000 human-written AI model cards on Hugging Face against real-world incidents from the AI Incident Database, this research identified major documentation blindspots—developers rarely document risks associated with fraud, manipulation, and social engineering. To solve this, we introduced <strong>RiskRAG</strong>, an interactive Retrieval-Augmented Generation system that automatically drafts contextual, actionable risk reports for developers.
        </p>
    </div>
</div>

<h3>Research Overview</h3>

<p>
    Addressing AI model risks requires systematic risk communication and reporting across the AI lifecycle. Developers and practitioners often struggle to identify domain-specific risks when selecting pre-trained models. This research investigates the gap between developer-reported risks in documentation and real-world AI incidents, creating data-driven tooling to foster more deliberative, responsible deployment decisions.
</p>
